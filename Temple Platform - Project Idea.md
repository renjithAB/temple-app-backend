# Temple Platform: Project Idea

## The idea in one line

A single platform where devotees can discover temples, see the timings of every pooja and occasion, and book them, while each temple manages its own information and its own bookings.

## The problem

Temple information is scattered. Pooja timings are on notice boards, in WhatsApp forwards, or only known by word of mouth. Festival dates change every year and follow the traditional calendar, so devotees often find out late. Booking a pooja usually means visiting the temple or calling the office, and temple staff track bookings in paper registers. Devotees travelling to a new place have no easy way to find temples nearby and learn what is offered there.

## The solution

One place that holds every participating temple's details:

- Who the temple is dedicated to, where it is, and how to reach it
- Every pooja offered, with timings, days, and offerings
- Festivals and special occasions, with dates and the poojas held on them
- Booking of poojas and occasions in advance, with a clear confirmation

Devotees get one simple way to find, search, schedule, and book. Temples get one simple way to publish their information and see who is coming.

## Who uses it

**Guests** are visitors who only want to look. They can browse temples, read details, and search without creating an account.

**Devotees** are registered users. They can do everything a guest can, and also book poojas and occasions, keep a schedule, and see their booking history.

**Temple administrators** run one temple each. They keep that temple's details, poojas, timings, and occasions up to date, and see the bookings made for their temple.

**Platform owner** creates new temples on the platform and appoints each temple's administrators.

## How temples stay separate

Every temple owns its own data: its poojas, timings, occasions, bookings, and staff. One temple cannot see or change another temple's information. The platform simply gives all of them a common home, so a devotee can move from one temple to another in the same place.

## What success looks like

- A devotee can find any participating temple and know exactly what happens there and when.
- A devotee can book a pooja for a chosen date in a few steps and be sure it is confirmed.
- A temple office can see tomorrow's bookings at a glance, without paper registers.
- A new temple can be added to the platform and be live within a day.

## Starting point

Begin with one real temple, learn from how its devotees and staff actually use it, and then bring more temples on board.
